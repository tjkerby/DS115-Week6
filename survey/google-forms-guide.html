DS 115: Google Forms survey setup

The instructor has completed the survey: https://forms.gle/EtDPQS7N3yaDtz8f9. The wording below is the original proposed survey, retained for reference. It has not been compared with the live form.

This is the anonymous course feedback and live-question form. The five-question final reflection remains a separate Canvas assignment. No live Google Form has been created by this package.

Form title

DS 115: Course Feedback and Final Questions

Form description

Copy this text into the form description after configuring and checking anonymity:

Your final reflection asks you to think about what you have learned, what interests you, and what you would like to do next. This survey gives you a chance to share feedback about the course and submit a question for our final discussion.

This survey does not collect your name or email address. Please submit your final reflection separately through the assignment.

You may skip any question. The password for the last attendance quiz will appear after you submit.

Question 1

Type: Paragraph
Required: Off
Question title: Which course experience was most useful to you, and what made it useful?

Description: This might be a class discussion or activity, job-posting exploration, major comparison, advisor meeting, graduation plan, professional outreach, or something else.

Question 2

Type: Paragraph
Required: Off
Question title: What would you change to make this course more useful for future students?

Description: You might suggest something to add, spend more time on, shorten, or approach differently.

Question 3

Type: Paragraph
Required: Off
Question title: What question are you still trying to answer that you would like us to discuss today?

Description: You are welcome to use the question from your final reflection. Questions about careers, graduate school, academic preparation, or anything else related to the course are welcome. I may paraphrase your question during our discussion. Please leave out identifying details.

Question 4

Type: Paragraph
Required: Off
Question title: Is there anything else you would like me to know about your experience in DS 115?

Description: Leave blank.

Labels and access controls can vary by Google Workspace account. Check the form’s current Publish/Share responder settings. Test the responder link in a signed-out or private browser before describing the form as anonymous. If institutional policy prevents access without identifying respondents, revise the anonymity claim and resolve access before class. Do not collect individual identities through this form to record attendance. The separate attendance quiz handles that.

Confirmation message

Replace the bracketed placeholder with the actual quiz password before sharing the form. Do not put the actual password in the public course website or slides.

Thank you for your feedback!

The password for the last attendance quiz is: [INSERT ACTUAL QUIZ PASSWORD]

Please submit your final reflection separately through the Canvas assignment. We will discuss a selection of your questions before class ends.

In class

  1. Give students 3 minutes to begin notes for the final reflection, then about 5 minutes for this form.
  2. Share the published responder link, not the editor URL. Put it in Canvas or display it alongside the survey slide.
  3. Confirm that a submission with all questions blank still reaches the password message.
  4. Read responses privately and paraphrase selected discussion questions. Keep response summaries, timestamps, and course feedback off the projector.
  5. Leave about 10 minutes for questions. Students may refine their final reflection after hearing the discussion.
