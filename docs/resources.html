Sources and Further Reading

Sources reviewed October 19, 2026. Job postings and program requirements can change.

Education and research work

The National Survey of College Graduates, 2023, Table 3-1, published by NSF’s National Center for Science and Engineering Statistics, reports employed graduates by highest degree, occupation, and primary work activity.

For the computer and mathematical scientist occupation group:

Highest degree Total employed R&D as primary activity Calculated share
Bachelor’s 2,989,000 357,000 11.94%
Master’s 1,353,000 174,000 12.86%
Doctorate 211,000 72,000 34.12%

Calculated share = R&D count divided by total employed, multiplied by 100. The lecture rounds these shares to 12%, 13%, and 34%. The published population estimates are themselves rounded to the nearest 1,000.

Interpretation

  • R&D includes basic research, applied research, and development.
  • The group includes computer and information scientists, mathematical scientists, and postsecondary computer and math teachers. It is broader than data science and spans employment sectors.
  • Primary activity is different from participation in research at any point in one’s work.
  • These estimates show an association. They do not establish what earning a degree causes.
  • The table does not measure leadership, research independence, or autonomy. Interests, specialization, employment sector, and experience may help explain differences.

Experience and technical leadership

Google Careers: Senior Staff Product Data Scientist, Merchant Shopping

As accessed October 19, 2026, this posting lists a relevant bachelor’s degree and 13 years of experience, or 10 years with a master’s, among its minimum qualifications. A master’s is also a preferred qualification. Responsibilities include guiding technical development, designing statistical infrastructure, and mentoring senior colleagues.

This is one example of an advertised route to substantial technical responsibility without a PhD. It does not establish typical qualifications, the degree of the eventual hire, or the causal value of additional education. The link may expire.

Research outside universities

Stanford HAI: 2026 AI Index, Research and Development

The report states that industry produced over 90% of notable AI models in 2025. That is evidence about notable model development, not a measurement of all AI research or all data science research. The lecture uses it to introduce the role of industry and the importance of evaluating a particular research environment.

Occupational profiles

  • Data Scientists, U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: responsibilities and typical entry education, including employer variation.
  • Mathematicians and Statisticians, BLS: work across settings and typical education for statisticians.
  • Computer and Information Research Scientists, BLS: research work in government, industry, and universities.
  • Postsecondary Teachers, BLS: typical doctoral preparation for four-year university faculty and the dissertation’s original research requirement.

These profiles describe broad patterns. Read actual responsibilities and qualifications when exploring a particular position.
